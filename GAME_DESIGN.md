# Game Design Document — Ella's Adventure

## Core Loop
1. **Dress Up**: Start directly in the Wardrobe with a live animated character preview.
2. **Play**: Press the big green **PLAY!** button to launch into the selected world.
3. **Run & Explore**: Automatic forward running with forgiving jumps, double jumps, slides, boost pads, and elevated bonus paths.
4. **Interact & Collect**: Gather stars, gems, and flowers; stop at Giraffe Feeding Stations in World 2 to feed friendly giraffes; discover 3 original creatures per world.
5. **Unlock & Celebrate**: Complete levels to unlock special outfits, hats, and winged shoes, then return to Dress Up!

## Child-Friendly Design Principles
- **No Punishing Failure**: Bumping an obstacle triggers a gentle "boing" bounce and brief invulnerability rather than Game Over.
- **Minimal Reading Required**: Large icons, color swatches, emoji badges, and optional Web Speech voice hints.
- **Speed Control**: Cozy (`0.8x`), Fast (`1.0x`), and Super (`1.25x`) speeds selectable anytime.
